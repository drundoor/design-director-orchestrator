# Peer Compact Checks

Use this when Impeccable or Hallmark is unavailable, or when recording compact
evidence before the final design-quality gate.

## Impeccable Fallback

Record outcomes for every applicable check:

- craft completeness: first screen has a coherent product-specific structure.
- bolder anti-generic: result rejects generic card walls, decorative pills, fake
  chrome, and vague "clean modern" styling.
- distinctiveness: screenshots show at least two strong non-generic choices
  from the distinctiveness floor, not just safe panels and pleasant spacing.
- domain specificity: a visible artifact, map, instrument, timeline, cutaway,
  workbench, or content structure makes the surface hard to relabel for another
  product.
- interaction/dynamism: an active state, motion/reveal, spatial comparison, or
  static-dynamic substitute is named and inspected.
- layout hierarchy: primary decision/action is visually dominant and related
  items stay close.
- typeset consistency: equivalent values and labels share type roles.
- adapt: mobile/tablet/desktop preserve hierarchy without clipping or overflow.
- harden: active states, focus, keyboard, touch, empty/error/loading states, and
  reduced motion are considered when relevant.
- clarify: labels, statuses, and helper copy are specific enough to act on.

## Hallmark Fallback

Record outcomes for every applicable check:

- generic scaffold rejected.
- decorative pills/chips/badges removed unless they carry real state.
- stock or fake decorative chrome removed.
- visual signature is visible in the first meaningful viewport.
- domain-specific artifact or interaction is visible, not merely described in
  the brief.
- hierarchy and spacing are intentional, not equal-weight filler.
- result does not look like a template after labels are swapped.

Fallback evidence must be written as a real markdown section with observed
outcomes. A TODO checklist is not evidence.
